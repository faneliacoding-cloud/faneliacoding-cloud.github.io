Overview

Rescoping vision into reality

Rescoped location strategy for the Spring 2024 campaign to optimize time, budget, and creative output, resulting in expanded asset capture within a single production day.

On Set

Campaign in Motion

When the original museum location fell through days before the shoot, we pivoted quickly — securing a nearby modernist home that offered greater flexibility for both the creative and production teams.

The shift expanded our shooting window, enabling greater asset capture and a more dynamic visual approach integrating movement, environment, and architecture.

Campaign Rollout

From Set to Street

Hero image featured across taxi tops and LinkNYC displays. Campaign imagery extended into a live brand environment, supported by casting I led — prioritizing a relatable, diverse mix of talent reflective of the collection's real-world audience.
