Meet Anna

About

You have a story worth telling.
I help brands figure out what it is.

I began with an editorial eye, shaping stories through image and narrative, and evolved into a leader in large-scale production and visual direction across fashion and lifestyle brands. That progression — from Editor to Editorial Director to Creative Lead — expanded how I see: not just the image, but the system behind it.

Today, I operate as a creative partner, aligning teams and guiding work from concept through execution with clarity and intention. I collaborate closely with creative, brand, and marketing teams to refine direction, strengthen narrative, and deliver work that resonates.

And when I'm not producing or directing, I'm often behind the lens, drawn to light, quiet moments, and the stories found in everyday spaces. That perspective continues to shape how I see, create, and lead.

02

The best creative work happens when everyone feels ownership. I build trust with teams, agencies, and clients so ideas can be challenged, refined, and strengthened.
