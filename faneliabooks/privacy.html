Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

FaneliaBooks ("we," "us," or "our") operates faneliabooks.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website and make purchases.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe. We never store your card details.
  • Email address — provided during checkout for order confirmation and delivery.
  • Name and billing address — collected by Stripe for payment processing.

We also automatically collect limited technical data such as your browser type, device type, and pages visited (via Google Analytics and AdSense).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your ebook purchases
  • To send order confirmation and download access emails
  • To improve our website and customer experience
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdSense

3. Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. We do not store, process, or have access to your credit card or payment information. Please review Stripe's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle payment data.

4. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ad Settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • Stripe — payment processing (Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdSense — advertising (Privacy Policy)
  • Cloudflare — hosting and CDN (Privacy Policy)

6. Data Retention

We retain purchase records as required by law (typically 7 years for financial records). You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or California, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal data (we do not sell data)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

8. Children's Privacy

FaneliaBooks is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
