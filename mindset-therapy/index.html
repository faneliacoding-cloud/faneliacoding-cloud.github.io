Meet Your Therapist

Aby Chacko, LCSW

There's something quietly profound about finally feeling understood. That's the space I aim to create for every person who walks through my door.

I'm Aby Chacko, a Licensed Clinical Social Worker with over a decade of experience helping individuals, teens, and families navigate some of life's most challenging seasons. My practice is grounded in Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), and mindfulness-based approaches — frameworks that are both clinically proven and deeply human.

I work with people facing anxiety, depression, trauma, grief, ADHD, and major life transitions. I believe that healing is not a linear path, and that each person carries within them a remarkable capacity for growth — sometimes they just need the right support to find it.

My practice is proudly LGBTQ+ affirming, culturally sensitive, and trauma-informed. Whatever brings you here, you will be received with dignity, compassion, and unwavering respect.
