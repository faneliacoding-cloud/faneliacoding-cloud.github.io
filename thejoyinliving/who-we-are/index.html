Founder & Lead Therapist

Claudia Christina Soddano

LCSW · Licensed Clinical Social Worker · Psychotherapist

With over 20 years of experience in the mental health field, Claudia Christina Soddano is the founder of The Joy In Living, LCSW, PLLC — a minority woman-owned practice committed to providing compassionate, culturally responsive mental health care in both English and Spanish.

Claudia's integrative approach honors the whole person — mind, body, and spirit. She combines evidence-based psychotherapy modalities with holistic wellness strategies to create lasting, meaningful change in the lives of her clients.

Our Approach

Integrative, Holistic, Person-Centered

Evidence-Based Therapy

We utilize proven therapeutic modalities tailored to each client's unique needs:

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) — Identifying and reshaping thought patterns that contribute to emotional distress
  • Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) — Building skills for emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and interpersonal effectiveness
  • Psychodynamic Therapy — Exploring the roots of emotional patterns through deeper self-understanding
  • Motivational Interviewing — Supporting clients in finding their own motivation for positive change

Holistic Wellness Integration

We believe healing extends beyond traditional talk therapy. Our integrative approach incorporates:

  • Breathwork — Calming the nervous system through guided breathing techniques
  • Trauma-Informed Yoga — Reconnecting with the body in safe, empowering ways
  • Meditation & Mindfulness — Cultivating present-moment awareness and inner calm
  • Journaling — Processing emotions and gaining clarity through reflective writing
