Family & Couples Therapy Serving Port Chester

Located just minutes from Port Chester in Sleepy Hollow, New York, The Joy In Living, LCSW, PLLC provides compassionate, evidence-based family and couples therapy to residents throughout southern Westchester County — including Port Chester, Rye, Greenwich, White Plains, and the surrounding area.

Founded by Christina Soddano, LCSW, our practice brings over 20 years of clinical expertise and a deep commitment to culturally sensitive, bilingual (English & Spanish) care. We believe every family deserves a therapist who truly understands their background, values, and lived experience.

Couples & Family Therapy

Strengthening Relationships in Port Chester & Beyond

Every couple and family faces challenges. Whether you're navigating communication breakdowns, rebuilding trust after betrayal, or supporting your children through a difficult transition — therapy can provide the structure, safety, and tools to move forward together.

Common reasons families in Port Chester seek therapy:

  • Communication breakdowns and recurring conflict
  • Infidelity and trust repair
  • Parenting disagreements and co-parenting after separation
  • Blended family and step-family challenges
  • Grief and loss affecting the whole family
  • Cultural and generational differences
  • Pre-marital preparation and relationship strengthening
  • Divorce support and transition planning

Our integrative approach draws on Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT), Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), and Psychodynamic methods — tailored specifically to your family's unique dynamic and goals.

Available in English & Spanish · In-Person at Sleepy Hollow & Telehealth · $250/session (couples)

Frequently Asked Questions — Port Chester Families

How far is your office from Port Chester?

Our office at 239 North Broadway, Suite LL 101, Sleepy Hollow, NY 10591 is approximately 10–15 minutes from Port Chester by car via I-287 or Route 9. Telehealth is also available for clients throughout New York State.

Do you offer therapy in Spanish?

Yes. Our founder Christina Soddano is fully bilingual and offers all services — individual, couples, and family therapy — in both English and Spanish. We serve many Spanish-speaking families in the Port Chester and lower Westchester area.

Do you accept insurance?

We accept Aetna, BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, MVP Health Care, Optum, Oscar Health, Oxford, and UnitedHealthcare. We also partner with Headway for streamlined insurance billing. For out-of-network plans, we provide super bills for reimbursement.

What is the cost of family therapy?

Couples and family therapy sessions are $250 per session. Individual therapy is $150 per session. A free 15-minute phone consultation is always available for new clients.

How do I get started?

Call us at (914) 758-5286 or email reception@thejoyinliving.com to Make An Appointment. We'll discuss your needs, answer your questions, and find a time that works for your family.
