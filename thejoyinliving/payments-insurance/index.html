Out-of-Network Coverage

If your insurance plan is not listed above, you may still be eligible for reimbursement through your out-of-network benefits. We provide super bills (detailed receipts) that you can submit to your insurance company for potential reimbursement.

We recommend calling your insurance provider to ask about your out-of-network mental health benefits before your first appointment.
