Expert Couples Therapy for Westchester Relationships

Every relationship goes through difficult seasons. At The Joy In Living, we provide a safe, non-judgmental space for couples to work through challenges, rebuild trust, and rediscover the connection that brought them together.

Our couples therapy is available in English and Spanish — making it accessible to the full diversity of Westchester County families.

Couples therapy can help with:

  • Communication breakdown and recurring arguments
  • Emotional distance and disconnection
  • Rebuilding trust after infidelity
  • Parenting disagreements
  • Intimacy and sexual concerns
  • Pre-marital preparation and strengthening
  • Blended family challenges
  • Separation and divorce navigation
  • Cultural and generational differences in relationships

Our Approach to Couples Therapy

We use an integrative approach, drawing from:

  • Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) — Strengthen your emotional bond and attachment security
  • Gottman Method principles — Build communication skills and manage conflict constructively
  • Psychodynamic Couples Therapy — Understand how individual histories shape relationship patterns
  • Culturally Responsive Practice — Honor your background, values, and family system

Frequently Asked Questions

Do both partners need to come to every session?

We typically start with both partners attending together. Depending on your goals, individual sessions may also be recommended alongside couples sessions.

How long does couples therapy take?

It varies by couple and goals. Many couples see meaningful progress in 8–12 sessions, while others benefit from longer-term support. We'll discuss your expectations in the first session.

Do you offer therapy in Spanish for couples?

Yes. All couples sessions are available in English or Spanish. Both partners can speak their preferred language — we accommodate multilingual sessions.

Does insurance cover couples therapy?

Coverage varies by plan. We accept major insurers and can help you understand your benefits. Self-pay rate is $250/session. Super bills available for out-of-network reimbursement.
