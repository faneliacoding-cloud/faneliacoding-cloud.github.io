🚨 Crisis & Emergency Resources

If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, please use these resources right away.

🔴 Emergency: Call 911
📞 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988 (24/7, en español disponible)
💬 Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
🏠 National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233
👶 Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline: 1-800-422-4453
