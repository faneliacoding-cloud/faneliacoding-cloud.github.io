Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

The Joy In Living, LCSW, PLLC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website at thejoyinliving.com.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — When you submit a contact form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and any message you provide.
  • Usage data — We may collect non-personally identifiable information such as browser type, pages visited, and time spent on pages.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule consultations
  • To improve our website and services
  • To comply with legal and ethical obligations

HIPAA Notice

As a mental health practice, we are bound by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). Any protected health information (PHI) shared during the course of treatment is handled in accordance with our separate HIPAA Notice of Privacy Practices, which you will receive upon becoming a client.

This website contact form is not a HIPAA-secure channel. Please do not share sensitive health information through this form.

Cookies

Our website may use minimal cookies to ensure basic functionality. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Therapy Portal — for secure client portal access
  • Headway — for insurance billing
  • Google Fonts — for typography

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To make such a request, please contact us at reception@thejoyinliving.com.
