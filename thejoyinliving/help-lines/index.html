Mental Health & Suicide Prevention

Mental Health Crisis Lines

🆘
988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
The national lifeline for people in suicidal crisis or mental health distress. Available 24/7 in English and Spanish. Counselors are trained, confidential, and compassionate.
📞 Call or Text 988 💬 Chat: 988lifeline.org
💬
Crisis Text Line
Text-based crisis support for anyone in emotional distress. Available 24/7. Private and confidential. No phone call required.
💬 Text HOME to 741741
❤️
Trevor Project (LGBTQ+ Youth)
Crisis intervention and suicide prevention for LGBTQ+ young people under 25. Available 24/7.
📞 1-866-488-7386 💬 Text START to 678-678
🧠
NAMI Helpline
National Alliance on Mental Illness information helpline for people experiencing a mental health crisis, seeking treatment resources, or supporting a loved one.
📞 1-800-950-6264 💬 Text NAMI to 741741
Domestic Violence & Safety

Domestic Violence & Safety Lines

🛡️
National Domestic Violence Hotline
Confidential support for victims of domestic violence and their families. Available 24/7 in English and Spanish with real-time translator access for 200+ languages.
📞 1-800-799-7233 💬 Text START to 88788 🌐 thehotline.org
🏠
Westchester County DV Services
Local crisis intervention, emergency shelter, and advocacy for domestic violence survivors in Westchester County.
📞 (914) 835-8010
⚖️
RAINN Sexual Assault Hotline
Routes callers to local sexual assault service providers and offers confidential support. Available 24/7.
📞 1-800-656-4673

Substance Use & Recovery Support

SAMHSA National Helpline
Free, confidential treatment referral and information for substance use disorders and mental health. Available 24/7 in English and Spanish.

NY State HOPEline
New York State's opioid and substance use helpline. Crisis counseling, referrals, and information.
📞 1-877-484-6454
