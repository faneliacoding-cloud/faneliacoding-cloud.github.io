Before You Join

🔇

Find a Private Space

Choose a quiet room where you won't be interrupted or overheard. Headphones are highly recommended.

📶

Strong Internet

Connect via Wi-Fi or ethernet. Close other apps and tabs to free up bandwidth.

🎙️

Test Audio & Video

Test your camera and microphone before joining. Button below takes you to the test page.

📱

Any Device Works

Works on smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. No download required.

Important Guidelines

🚫

Recording of sessions is strictly prohibited by either party. Sessions are confidential and protected by HIPAA.

📍

Telehealth is only available to clients physically located in New York State at the time of the session.

🆘

If you experience a crisis during a session, call 911 immediately or go to your nearest emergency room.
