For New Clients

How to Get Started

Your first therapy experience should feel warm and simple — not complicated. Here's how easy it is to begin.

1

Free Phone Consultation

Call (914) 686-2484 or email reception@thejoyinliving.com for a free 15-minute call. We'll discuss your needs, answer questions, and ensure we're a good fit.

2

Complete Your Intake Paperwork

Once scheduled, you'll receive a secure link to your patient portal. Complete your intake forms online at your own pace — before your first session.

3

Attend Your First Session

Join us in person at our Sleepy Hollow office (239 North Broadway, Suite LL 101) or via telehealth. Your first session is a chance to share your story and begin your healing journey.

Telehealth Tips

Get the Most from Your Virtual Session

🔇

Find Privacy

Choose a quiet space where you won't be interrupted or overheard. Headphones help.

📶

Strong Connection

Wi-Fi or ethernet is preferred. Close unnecessary tabs and apps for best video quality.

🎙️

Test First

Use the "Test Audio & Video" button in the portal before every session.

🚫

No Recording

Sessions are confidential. Recording of any kind is not permitted by either party.
