Meet Your Therapist

Claudia Christina Soddano, LCSW

"I believe in meeting each person where they are, with compassion, cultural understanding, and a commitment to their healing journey."

With over 20 years of experience in mental health, Claudia brings a warm, integrative approach that combines evidence-based psychotherapy with holistic wellness strategies. A bilingual Latina clinician, she creates a culturally sensitive, safe space for clients of all backgrounds.

Her approach integrates Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), Psychodynamic therapy, and Motivational Interviewing — complemented by breathwork, trauma-informed yoga, meditation, and journaling.
