Step by Step

Your Journey With Us

From your first phone call to your ongoing sessions, here's what to expect every step of the way.

1

Book Your Free Consultation

Call us at (914) 686-2484 or email reception@thejoyinliving.com for your free 15-minute phone consultation. We'll discuss your needs, answer questions, and see if we're a good fit.

2

Complete Your Paperwork

You'll receive access to our Patient Portal (TherapyPortal) where you can complete intake forms, consent documents, and provide relevant history — all securely online before your first session.

3

Initial Assessment Session

Your first session is a comprehensive assessment (45-50 minutes). We'll get to know you, understand your concerns, explore your goals, and create a personalized treatment plan together. There's no pressure — this is your space.

4

Begin Regular Sessions

Based on your treatment plan, we'll schedule ongoing sessions (typically weekly to start). Sessions can be in-person at our Sleepy Hollow office or via secure telehealth — whatever works best for you.

5

Ongoing Support & Growth

As you progress, we'll adjust frequency and approach together. We celebrate your growth and are here for every step of your healing journey.
