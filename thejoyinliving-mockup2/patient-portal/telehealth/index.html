Tips for a Great Session

1
Test your setup early
Log in 5 minutes before your session to check your camera, microphone, and internet connection.
2
Find a quiet, private space
A room where you can speak freely without interruption makes the biggest difference in session quality.
3
Use headphones if possible
Headphones reduce echo and improve audio clarity for both you and your therapist.
4
Have a backup plan
If video isn't working, we can switch to a phone call. Keep our number handy: (914) 686-2484
