Meet Your Therapist

Claudia Christina Soddano, LCSW

"I believe in meeting each person where they are — with compassion, cultural understanding, and a deep commitment to their healing."

With over 20 years of experience in mental health, Claudia brings a warm, integrative approach that combines evidence-based psychotherapy with holistic wellness. A bilingual Latina clinician, she creates a culturally sensitive, safe space for clients of all backgrounds.

Her approach integrates CBT, DBT, Psychodynamic therapy, and Motivational Interviewing — complemented by breathwork, trauma-informed yoga, meditation, and journaling.

NY Licensed LCSW CBT · DBT · Psychodynamic Bilingual EN/ES PHR Trained Trauma-Informed Minority Woman-Owned
