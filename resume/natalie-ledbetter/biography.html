Professional Biography
Natalie Ledbetter
Senior Executive Assistant  ·  Executive Business Partner  ·  Wealth Management & Alternative Investments

Natalie Ledbetter is a distinguished Executive Business Partner and Senior Executive Assistant with more than twelve years of service at the pinnacle of the global financial services industry. Recognized for her exceptional ability to operate at the highest levels of institutional finance, Natalie has served as the trusted right hand to Presidents, CEOs, Co-Heads, Managing Partners, and Managing Directors across some of the world's most elite alternative investment, wealth management, and investment banking organizations.

Natalie's career is defined by a rare breadth of executive support expertise spanning wealth management, private equity, private credit, credit secondaries, and alternative investments. Her institutional pedigree includes pivotal roles at Evercore Wealth Management, where she currently supports the President and CEO alongside two Co-Head Partners; Ares Management; Apollo Global Management; Neuberger Berman; L Catterton; Hutchin Hill Capital; and Bloomberg LP — a portfolio of firms collectively representing hundreds of billions of dollars in assets under management and some of the most recognizable names in global institutional finance.

Throughout her career, Natalie has demonstrated an extraordinary capacity for managing complexity — simultaneously supporting as many as seven senior investment professionals while maintaining the standard of operational precision and executive presence expected in the world's most demanding financial environments. She is widely recognized as a strategic gatekeeper, an operational leader, and a confidential advisor whose judgment is implicitly trusted by the leaders she serves.

Natalie's executive support expertise encompasses strategic calendar management, international travel program oversight, investor meeting coordination, board-level logistics, executive expense management, and cross-functional stakeholder engagement. She is deeply fluent in the enterprise technology platforms that define modern financial services operations — including Concur, Workday, Salesforce, ServiceNow, Bloomberg Terminal, SAP, PeopleSoft, and the full Microsoft 365 suite — and brings a sophisticated command of AI-enhanced productivity tools that elevate her operational effectiveness in a rapidly evolving digital environment.

Known for her composure under pressure, her institutional discretion, and her ability to anticipate the needs of the leaders she serves before they arise, Natalie Ledbetter represents the highest standard of executive partnership in financial services. She is sought after by recruiters and executives alike for her ability to bring order to complexity, elevate the executive experience, and serve as an indispensable operational force within the most demanding investment organizations in the world.
