Mini B.™

For babies, every day is full of motion and discovery — from morning stretches to playtime in the park to blissful naps and afternoon giggles, Mini B. keeps them comfy from sunrise to sunset.

The fun and fashionable products from this Baby Starters sister brand are made for babies' daily activities. The mix and match styles and multi-piece sets, featuring unique designs with everyday whimsy, are easy-to-wear (and easy to care). Mini B. offers convenience and modern design, all made with soft, comfortable fabrics at a great price that don't sacrifice quality. Because everyday essentials don't have to be ordinary!
