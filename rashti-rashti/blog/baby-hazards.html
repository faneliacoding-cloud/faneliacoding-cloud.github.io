Heads Up: How to Minimize Potential Hazards During Your Baby's First Year

By Pamela Brill

As your baby grows and becomes more mobile, the world becomes their playground—and potential hazard zone. Staying one step ahead with childproofing can prevent accidents.

Get Down to Their Level

Crawl around your home to see things from your baby's perspective. You'll spot dangers you might miss from standing height—sharp corners, loose cords, and small objects within reach.

Secure Heavy Items

Anchor bookshelves, TVs, and dressers to the wall. Toppling furniture is one of the most common and dangerous household hazards for babies and toddlers.

Kitchen and Bathroom Safety

Install cabinet locks, keep cleaning supplies out of reach, and never leave a child unattended near water. These simple precautions can prevent the most common household accidents.
