Parents Corner

Last-Minute Father's Day Gift Guide

By Pamela Brill

Father's Day is just around the corner and you still haven't found the perfect gift? Don't worry—we've got you covered with ideas that are thoughtful, quick, and sure to make Dad smile.

Experience Over Things

Plan an outing he'll love—tickets to a game, a round of golf, or a family hike followed by his favorite takeout. Memories last longer than material gifts.

DIY from the Kids

Nothing melts Dad's heart like a handmade card, a painted picture, or a "coupon book" of free hugs and breakfast in bed. Help the little ones get creative.

Practical Luxuries

Upgrade something he uses every day—a nice wallet, premium coffee subscription, or comfortable loungewear. Practical gifts that feel a little luxurious always hit the mark.
