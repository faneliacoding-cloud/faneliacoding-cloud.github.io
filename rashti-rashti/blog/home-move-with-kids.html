Home Sweet Home: Managing a Move with Young Kids

By Pamela Brill

Moving to a new home is stressful enough without adding little ones to the mix. With some planning and patience, you can make the transition smoother for the whole family.

Prepare Them Early

Talk to your kids about the move well in advance. Show them pictures of the new house, neighborhood, and nearby parks. The more familiar the new environment feels, the less anxious they'll be.

Keep Routines Intact

Amid the chaos of packing and unpacking, try to maintain your children's regular schedules for meals, naps, and bedtime. Consistency provides comfort during times of change.

Set Up Their Room First

Make your child's room a priority when unpacking. Having their familiar belongings—favorite toys, blankets, and books—arranged in their new space helps them feel at home faster.
