Healthy Eating for the Holidays…and Beyond

By Pamela Brill

With holiday treats at every turn, maintaining healthy eating habits can feel impossible. But with a balanced approach, you can enjoy the season without derailing your family's nutrition.

Don't Skip Meals

Arriving at a holiday gathering starving leads to overeating. Have a healthy snack before parties and keep regular mealtimes for the kids.

Bring a Healthy Dish

When attending gatherings, contribute a nutritious option so you know there's at least one healthy choice available for your family.

Practice the 80/20 Rule

Aim for healthy choices 80% of the time and allow indulgences the other 20%. This balanced approach removes the guilt from occasional treats and teaches kids that all foods can fit into a healthy lifestyle.
