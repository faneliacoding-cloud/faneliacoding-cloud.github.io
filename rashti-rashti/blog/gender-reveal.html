Let’s Hear it for the Boy (or Girl): Hosting a Gender-Reveal Party

By Pamela Brill

Gender-reveal parties have become one of the most anticipated celebrations during pregnancy. Whether you’re team blue or team pink (or team green!), here’s how to host a memorable gathering.

Choose Your Reveal

From cutting into a colored cake to popping confetti-filled balloons, the reveal itself should be fun and shareable. Keep it safe and simple—the best reveals are the ones that focus on joy, not spectacle.

Set the Scene

Create a festive atmosphere with themed decorations. Think neutral colors with pops of pink and blue, a photo booth with fun props, and a dessert table that doubles as décor.

Make it Meaningful

Remember, this celebration is about the love growing in your family. Include guests in guessing games, set up a wishes-for-baby station, and capture every moment. Whether you find out the sex or keep it a surprise, what matters most is the community of love surrounding your little one.
