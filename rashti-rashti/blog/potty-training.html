Potty Training 101: How to Get Your Toddler to Go, Like a Champ

By Pamela Brill

Potty training is one of those parenting milestones that can feel daunting, but with patience, consistency, and the right approach, your toddler will be diaper-free before you know it.

Watch for Readiness Signs

Most children show readiness between 18 months and 3 years. Signs include staying dry for longer periods, showing interest in the bathroom, and being able to follow simple instructions. Don’t rush it—every child develops at their own pace.

Create a Routine

Set regular potty times—after meals, before bath, and first thing in the morning. Use a small, child-sized potty and let your toddler pick out fun underwear as motivation.

Celebrate Successes

Positive reinforcement works wonders. Cheer, clap, or use a sticker chart to celebrate every success. And when accidents happen (they will!), stay calm and encouraging. Potty training is a marathon, not a sprint.
